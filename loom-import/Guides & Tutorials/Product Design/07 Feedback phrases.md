# Feedback phrases

Part of [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

The first output is a draft. Designers steer with short, specific critique. Copy a phrase and fill any brackets.

- **Self-review**: Critique your own design against Nielsen's 10 heuristics, then fix the top 3 issues.
- **Hierarchy**: Tighten the hierarchy. [Element] should be read first, then [element], then everything else.
- **Reduce noise**: This feels busy. Remove anything that doesn't serve the primary task, and move secondary actions behind progressive disclosure.
- **Density**: Increase information density for frequent users without shrinking touch targets below 44 pt.
- **Rhythm**: Align everything to an 8-point spacing scale and list the values you used.
- **States**: Show me the empty, loading, error and success states for this screen.
- **Edge cases**: What happens with a very long title, zero items, and 1,000 items? Show each.
- **Mobile reach**: Move the primary action into the thumb zone and keep it visible above the keyboard.
- **Friction**: Replace the confirmation dialog with an undo toast.
- **Overlays**: Use a bottom sheet instead of a full-screen modal so the user keeps context.
- **Copy**: Rewrite all labels to be verb-first and in the user's words, not system terms.
- **Responsive**: Show this at 375, 768 and 1280 px wide and list what changes.
- **Divergence**: Give me two more directions that differ in layout, not just colour.
- **Tone**: Keep the layout but make the tone calmer: fewer colours, more whitespace, lighter weights.
- **Accessibility**: Check every text and background pair for WCAG AA contrast in both themes and fix failures.
- **Rationale**: Explain why you chose this layout over the obvious alternative.
