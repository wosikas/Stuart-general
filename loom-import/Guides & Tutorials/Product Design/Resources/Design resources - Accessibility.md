# Design resources - Accessibility

Part of [[11 Resource library]] in the [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

Standards, checklists and checkers, so that everyone can use what you build.

16 links. A star marks an essential. Last link check: 18 November 2026.

- **[WCAG 2.2 Quick Reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/)** ★ (W3C; guideline; free). Every success criterion, filterable by level, with techniques for meeting each. The source you cite in audits. *In a prompt:* "Audit against WCAG 2.2 AA and cite the success criterion number for each issue."
- **[Designing for Web Accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tips/designing/)** ★ (W3C WAI; guideline; free). Ten illustrated tips that cover most accessibility issues a designer controls. *In a prompt:* "Check this design against the W3C's ten tips for designing for accessibility."
- **[The A11Y Project Checklist](https://www.a11yproject.com/checklist/)** (The A11Y Project; reference; free). A plain-language checklist organised by content, layout, forms, media and controls. *In a prompt:* "Run this page through the A11Y Project checklist and report failures."
- **[ARIA Authoring Practices Guide: Patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/)** (W3C; guideline; free). The correct keyboard and screen-reader behaviour for every common widget: dialog, tabs, combobox, menu. *In a prompt:* "Implement the dialog to the ARIA Authoring Practices pattern, including focus trap."
- **[WebAIM Contrast Checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/)** (WebAIM; tool; free). Enter two colours and see whether they pass at each level and text size. *In a prompt:* "List every text and background pair with its contrast ratio."
- **[Who Can Use](https://www.whocanuse.com/)** (Corey Ginnivan; tool; free). Shows how a colour pair looks to people with each type of vision, and how many people that is. *In a prompt:* "Check the palette under the common colour-vision deficiencies."
- **[Inclusive Components](https://inclusive-components.design/)** (Heydon Pickering; reference; free). Deep dives into building one component at a time accessibly: toggles, menus, tooltips, cards. *In a prompt:* "Build the toggle as Inclusive Components recommends, with the correct role and state."
- **[Microsoft Inclusive Design](https://inclusive.microsoft.design/)** (Microsoft; guideline; free). The permanent, temporary and situational disability model: designing for one arm also serves a parent holding a child. *In a prompt:* "List permanent, temporary and situational constraints for this feature."
- **[Apple HIG: Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility)** (Apple; guideline; free). VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, contrast, reduced motion and target sizes on Apple platforms. *In a prompt:* "Support VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type up to the largest size, and Reduce Motion."
- **[Dos and don'ts on designing for accessibility](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/2016/09/02/dos-and-donts-on-designing-for-accessibility/)** (UK Home Office; reference; free). Six posters of simple dos and don'ts for different groups of users. Easy to remember and to quote. *In a prompt:* "Apply the GOV.UK dos and don'ts for users with low vision and dyslexia."
- **[Learn Accessibility](https://web.dev/learn/accessibility)** (Google · web.dev; course; free). A free, thorough course covering everything from semantics to testing. *In a prompt:* "Explain how to test this page with a keyboard and a screen reader."
- **[axe DevTools](https://www.deque.com/axe/devtools/)** (Deque; tool; free tier). The standard browser extension for automated accessibility testing of built pages. *In a prompt:* "Fix every issue an axe scan would flag on this page."
- **[WAVE](https://wave.webaim.org/)** (WebAIM; tool; free). Paste a URL and see accessibility errors marked on the page itself. *In a prompt:* "Predict what WAVE would flag here: missing labels, contrast, heading order."
- **[Stark](https://www.getstark.co/)** (Stark; tool; free tier). Accessibility checks inside Figma and the browser: contrast, vision simulation, focus order. *In a prompt:* "Annotate focus order and landmarks on this design."
- **[Adrian Roselli](https://adrianroselli.com/)** (Adrian Roselli; blog; free). Rigorous, tested accessibility articles. The place to check whether a popular pattern really works with assistive technology. *In a prompt:* "Is this pattern actually accessible in practice, or only in theory?"
- **[Deque Blog](https://www.deque.com/blog/)** (Deque; blog; free). Accessibility news, legal updates and how-to guides from the makers of axe. *In a prompt:* "What accessibility requirements apply to this kind of product, and since when?"
