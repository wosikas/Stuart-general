# Design resources - Courses and practice

Part of [[11 Resource library]] in the [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

Books, courses and video channels for going deeper once the basics are in place.

21 links. A star marks an essential. Last link check: 18 November 2026.

- **[Practical UI](https://www.practical-ui.com/)** ★ (Adham Dannaway; book; paid). A logic-driven visual design handbook with clear rules and before-and-after examples. A strong companion to Refactoring UI. *In a prompt:* "Give a logical reason for every visual decision, not a taste judgement."
- **[Google UX Design Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-ux-design)** (Google · Coursera; course; paid). A structured beginner course through the whole process, from research to high-fidelity prototypes. *In a prompt:* "Walk me through this project the way a UX course capstone would."
- **[Interaction Design Foundation courses](https://ixdf.org/courses)** (IxDF; course; paid). University-style courses on every UX topic, for a flat membership fee. *In a prompt:* "Teach me this topic in five short lessons with an exercise each."
- **[Learn UI Design](https://www.learnui.design/)** (Erik Kennedy; course; paid). A thorough video course on visual design, taught through practical frameworks. *In a prompt:* "Critique this design the way a UI design tutor would, with specific fixes."
- **[Shift Nudge](https://shiftnudge.com/)** (Matt D. Smith; course; paid). A well-regarded interface design course focused on visual craft. *In a prompt:* "Push this layout from adequate to polished: what five nudges would you make?"
- **[Uxcel](https://uxcel.com/)** (Uxcel; course; free tier). Bite-sized interactive lessons and skill tests, good for a few minutes a day. *In a prompt:* "Quiz me on design vocabulary with ten multiple-choice questions."
- **[Design basics](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/design-basics/)** (Figma; course; free). A free introduction to design principles, colour, typography and layout. *In a prompt:* "Explain the design principle at work here in one paragraph."
- **[Daily UI](https://www.dailyui.co/)** (Daily UI; course; free). A design brief by email every weekday. Use each one as a prompt-writing exercise. *In a prompt:* "Today's brief: write the prompt first, then judge the output with the checklist."
- **[Hack Design](https://hackdesign.org/)** (Hack Design; course; free). A free curated curriculum of readings, one lesson a week. *In a prompt:* "Summarise this lesson's readings and give me one exercise."
- **[NN/g on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@NNgroup)** (Nielsen Norman Group; video; free). Hundreds of three-minute videos, each explaining one UX concept. *In a prompt:* "Explain this concept in three minutes, as an NN/g video would."
- **[Figma on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Figma)** (Figma; video; free). Tutorials and Config conference talks from leading design teams. *In a prompt:* "Summarise how a leading team approaches this, as a Config talk would."
- **[Steve Schoger on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@SteveSchoger)** (Steve Schoger; video; free). Screen recordings of real interfaces being redesigned, with the reasoning spoken aloud. *In a prompt:* "Redesign this screen step by step and narrate each change."
- **[Juxtopposed on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@juxtopposed)** (Juxtopposed; video; free). Popular videos that redesign well-known apps step by step, explaining each decision. *In a prompt:* "Redesign this screen and narrate the reasoning for each change."
- **[Mizko on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Mizko)** (Mizko; video; free). Videos on product design process, from research through to handoff. *In a prompt:* "Walk through the end-to-end design process for this feature."
- **[Malewicz on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MalewiczHype)** (Michal Malewicz; video; free). Opinionated, practical videos on UI design fundamentals and common mistakes. *In a prompt:* "What beginner UI mistakes does this screen make?"
- **[DesignCourse on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DesignCourse)** (Gary Simon; video; free). UI and UX tutorials with a front-end slant, good for people who also build. *In a prompt:* "Critique this layout and show the corrected version."
- **[Flux Academy on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@FluxAcademy)** (Flux Academy; video; free). Web design tutorials on layout, typography and visual polish. *In a prompt:* "Improve this web page's layout and type in five steps."
- **[Kevin Powell on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@KevinPowell)** (Kevin Powell; video; free). The clearest teacher of CSS. Useful for understanding what layouts are possible and what to call them. *In a prompt:* "Explain the simplest robust way to build this layout in CSS."
- **[Femke on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@femkedesign)** (Femke van Schoonhoven; video; free). A working product designer on process, critique and presenting work. *In a prompt:* "Help me present this design: the problem, the options, the decision."
- **[AJ&Smart on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AJSmart)** (AJ&Smart; video; free). Clear walkthroughs of design sprints and workshop exercises. *In a prompt:* "Run a lightning decision exercise on this problem."
- **[Google Design on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@GoogleDesign)** (Google; video; free). Talks and explainers from Google's design teams, including Material Design sessions. *In a prompt:* "Summarise the Material 3 guidance relevant to this component."
