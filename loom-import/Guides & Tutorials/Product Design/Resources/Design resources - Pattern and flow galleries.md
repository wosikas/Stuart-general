# Design resources - Pattern and flow galleries

Part of [[11 Resource library]] in the [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

Real screens from real products. Find a reference, then name it in your prompt.

26 links. A star marks an essential. Last link check: 18 November 2026.

- **[Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/)** ★ (Mobbin; gallery; free tier). The largest searchable library of real app screens and full flows, for iOS, Android and web. The best source of references to name in prompts. *In a prompt:* "Model the capture flow on how Things 3 and Bear handle quick entry."
- **[Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/)** (Page Flows; gallery; free tier). Screen recordings of complete user flows such as onboarding, upgrading and cancelling, from well-known products. *In a prompt:* "Compare our onboarding with three recorded flows and list the differences."
- **[Refero](https://refero.design/)** (Refero; gallery; free tier). A large, well-tagged library of web app screens, searchable by page type and component. *In a prompt:* "Find the common structure of settings pages and apply it here."
- **[Nicelydone](https://nicelydone.club/)** (Nicelydone; gallery; free tier). Flows and components from SaaS products, tagged by pattern. *In a prompt:* "How do leading SaaS products design their upgrade prompts?"
- **[UI Patterns](https://ui-patterns.com/patterns)** (Anders Toxboe; reference; free). A pattern library that explains the problem each pattern solves and when to use it, not just what it looks like. *In a prompt:* "Which named UI pattern solves this problem, and when should it not be used?"
- **[GOV.UK patterns](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/)** (UK Government Digital Service; guideline; free). Tested solutions to whole tasks: asking for names, dates and addresses, confirming, checking answers. *In a prompt:* "Use the GOV.UK 'check answers' pattern before the user submits."
- **[Baymard Institute](https://baymard.com/blog)** (Baymard Institute; reference; free tier). Large-scale usability research on forms, search, checkout and navigation. The benchmark for any form you design. *In a prompt:* "Apply Baymard's form findings: single column, labels above, inline validation."
- **[UserOnboard](https://www.useronboard.com/user-onboarding-teardowns/)** (Samuel Hulick; case studies; free). Slide-by-slide teardowns of how popular apps welcome new users. *In a prompt:* "Tear down our first-run experience screen by screen, in the UserOnboard style."
- **[Figma Community](https://www.figma.com/community)** (Figma; gallery; free). Free UI kits, wireframe kits, icon sets and templates you can copy and adapt. *In a prompt:* "Start from a community wireframe kit, not a blank canvas."
- **[Land-book](https://land-book.com/)** (Land-book; gallery; free). A curated gallery of landing pages, filterable by style and industry. *In a prompt:* "Give me three landing page structures used by notes and productivity apps."
- **[Recent](https://recent.design/)** (Recent (formerly Godly); gallery; free). High-end web design references, strong on motion and art direction. *In a prompt:* "Use this site's typographic restraint as the reference for our marketing page."
- **[Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/)** (Lapa Ninja; gallery; free). Thousands of landing pages with full-page screenshots, by category. *In a prompt:* "What sections do the best SaaS landing pages include, in what order?"
- **[Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/)** (Dribbble; gallery; free). Visual design shots. Good for style direction, weak for real usability, so treat it as mood, not evidence. *In a prompt:* "Borrow the visual tone of this shot, but keep the layout from our wireframe."
- **[Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/)** (Awwwards; gallery; free). Award-winning websites, judged on design, usability, creativity and content. The benchmark for ambitious web design. *In a prompt:* "Use this award-winning site as the reference for motion and art direction."
- **[Behance](https://www.behance.net/)** (Adobe; gallery; free). Full design projects and case studies from designers worldwide. *In a prompt:* "Present this project as a Behance-style case study."
- **[Layers](https://layers.to/)** (Layers; gallery; free). A community where product designers share work in progress and finished interfaces. *In a prompt:* "What are working product designers posting for this kind of screen?"
- **[Savee](https://savee.com/)** (Savee; gallery; free tier). A visual bookmarking tool popular with designers for building mood boards. *In a prompt:* "Describe a mood board for this brand: five images and what each contributes."
- **[Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so/)** (Cosmos; gallery; free tier). A quiet, ad-free space for collecting visual inspiration. *In a prompt:* "Define the visual world of this product in six reference images."
- **[Are.na](https://www.are.na/)** (Are.na; gallery; free tier). A tool for collecting and connecting references. Also a thoughtful example of a knowledge tool. *In a prompt:* "Gather references for this problem into themed groups."
- **[Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/)** (Minimal Gallery; gallery; free). Hand-picked minimal websites. Useful for calm, restrained design references. *In a prompt:* "Use a minimal reference: generous whitespace, one typeface, almost no colour."
- **[Curated](https://curated.design/)** (Curated; gallery; free). Web design inspiration from real live sites, filterable by type and style. *In a prompt:* "Find three live sites in this style and describe what they share."
- **[Dark Mode Design](https://www.darkmodedesign.com/)** (Dark Mode Design; gallery; free). Hand-picked dark-themed websites. Useful for seeing what good dark palettes look like. *In a prompt:* "Base the dark theme on these references, not on inverted light colours."
- **[One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/)** (Rob Hope; gallery; free). A long-running gallery of single-page websites and landing pages. *In a prompt:* "Structure this as a one-page site: the sections and their order."
- **[Landingfolio](https://www.landingfolio.com/)** (Landingfolio; gallery; free tier). Landing page examples broken down by section, such as hero, pricing and testimonials. *In a prompt:* "Show three strong pricing section layouts and choose one."
- **[SaaS Landing Page](https://saaslandingpage.com/)** (SaaS Landing Page; gallery; free). A gallery of landing pages from software companies. *In a prompt:* "What do the best SaaS landing pages put above the fold?"
- **[ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/)** (ScreensDesign; gallery; free tier). A library of mobile app screens and App Store screenshots. *In a prompt:* "Design App Store screenshots that explain the app in five frames."
