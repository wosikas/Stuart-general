# Design resources - Voices to follow

Part of [[11 Resource library]] in the [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

People, blogs and podcasts that keep you current.

31 links. A star marks an essential. Last link check: 18 November 2026.

- **[Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/)** (Lenny Rachitsky; blog; free tier). The most-read product newsletter. Deep interviews on product, growth and design practice. *In a prompt:* "How would a strong product team frame this decision?"
- **[LukeW](https://www.lukew.com/ff/)** ★ (Luke Wroblewski; blog; free). Two decades of short, evidence-led notes on forms, mobile and now AI interfaces. *In a prompt:* "Apply mobile-first thinking: what stays when the screen is small?"
- **[The Looking Glass](https://lg.substack.com/)** (Julie Zhuo; blog; free tier). Essays on design judgement and critique from Facebook's former head of design. *In a prompt:* "Run a design critique: what is this trying to do, and does it succeed?"
- **[The Linear Method](https://linear.app/method)** (Linear; guideline; free). How a team known for craft plans, scopes and builds. Short and opinionated. *In a prompt:* "Scope this to something we could ship well in one cycle."
- **[Brad Frost](https://bradfrost.com/blog/)** (Brad Frost; blog; free). Ongoing writing on design systems and, recently, on AI in design system work. *In a prompt:* "How should a design system change when AI generates the screens?"
- **[UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/)** (UX Collective; blog; free tier). A large, curated publication of practitioner essays and an annual state-of-UX report. *In a prompt:* "What are the current debates on this topic among practitioners?"
- **[Smashing Magazine](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/)** (Smashing Magazine; blog; free). Long, practical articles on design patterns, accessibility and front-end. *In a prompt:* "Give me the practical guidelines for this pattern, with edge cases."
- **[Sidebar](https://sidebar.io/)** (Sacha Greif; blog; free). Five good design links every day. A low-effort way to stay current. *In a prompt:* "What should I read this week to get better at judging interfaces?"
- **[Dense Discovery](https://www.densediscovery.com/)** (Kai Brach; blog; free). A thoughtful weekly on design, technology and culture. *In a prompt:* "What is the considered, humane version of this feature?"
- **[Figma Blog](https://www.figma.com/blog/)** (Figma; blog; free). Design practice, tool updates and interviews with working teams. *In a prompt:* "How are teams combining AI generation with their design systems?"
- **[Jakob Nielsen on UX](https://jakobnielsenphd.substack.com/)** (Jakob Nielsen; blog; free). The usability pioneer's current writing, much of it on AI and the future of UX work. *In a prompt:* "How does this classic usability rule change when the interface is conversational?"
- **[Design Tools Survey](https://uxtools.co/survey/)** (UX Tools; reference; free). An annual survey of which tools designers actually use for each job. *In a prompt:* "Which tools do most teams use for this job today?"
- **[Dive Club](https://www.dive.club/)** ★ (Ridd; podcast; free). Interviews with leading product designers about how they actually work, including how they use AI tools. *In a prompt:* "How are top product designers fitting AI tools into their process?"
- **[Design Details](https://designdetails.fm/)** (Brian Lovin & Marshall Bock; podcast; free). A long-running weekly conversation about design process, tools and careers. *In a prompt:* "What are the trade-offs working designers weigh on this kind of decision?"
- **[Design Better](https://designbetterpodcast.com/)** (Aarron Walter & Eli Woolery; podcast; free tier). Interviews on design, creativity and leadership with well-known practitioners. *In a prompt:* "How would an experienced design leader frame this problem?"
- **[UX Podcast](https://uxpodcast.com/)** (James Royal-Lawson & Per Axbom; podcast; free). A twice-monthly show on user experience and digital design, with thoughtful guests. *In a prompt:* "What ethical questions does this design raise?"
- **[Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/podcast)** (Lenny Rachitsky; podcast; free). Long interviews with product leaders and designers on how they build. *In a prompt:* "What would a seasoned product leader ask before approving this?"
- **[Center Centre articles](https://articles.centercentre.com/)** (Jared Spool; blog; free). Decades of influential writing on usability, design strategy and how organisations get better at UX. *In a prompt:* "What user outcome is this design meant to change, and how will we measure it?"
- **[Josh W. Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/)** (Josh W. Comeau; blog; free). Interactive tutorials on CSS, animation and interface details, beautifully explained. *In a prompt:* "Explain how to build this effect, and why it works."
- **[Ahmad Shadeed](https://ishadeed.com/)** (Ahmad Shadeed; blog; free). Detailed, illustrated articles on layout and how real interfaces behave when content changes. *In a prompt:* "How does this layout behave with long text, missing images and right-to-left languages?"
- **[Stéphanie Walter](https://stephaniewalter.design/blog/)** (Stéphanie Walter; blog; free). A UX researcher and designer's blog, with a weekly round-up of excellent links and many practical guides. *In a prompt:* "Give me a practical checklist for designing this for enterprise users."
- **[Adham Dannaway](https://www.adhamdannaway.com/blog)** (Adham Dannaway; blog; free). Short, logic-driven UI design tips with before-and-after examples. *In a prompt:* "Give a logical reason for each UI change, with a before and after."
- **[Matt Ström-Awn](https://mattstromawn.com/)** (Matt Ström-Awn; blog; free). Essays on design systems, colour science and design leadership. *In a prompt:* "What colour space should this palette be defined in, and why?"
- **[Frank Chimero](https://frankchimero.com/)** (Frank Chimero; blog; free). Reflective essays on design, the web and making things with care. *In a prompt:* "What would the more considered, less fashionable version of this look like?"
- **[Bret Victor](https://worrydream.com/)** (Bret Victor; reference; free). Essays and talks that question what interfaces could be. Demanding, and worth it. *In a prompt:* "What would this look like if the user could see the effect of every change immediately?"
- **[Scott Jenson](https://jenson.org/)** (Scott Jenson; blog; free). A veteran of Apple and Google on interface fundamentals and their history. *In a prompt:* "Which convention here exists only for historical reasons?"
- **[Dan Mall](https://danmall.com/)** (Dan Mall; blog; free). Writing on design systems and how designers and developers work together. *In a prompt:* "How should designer and developer work on this component at the same time?"
- **[Ethan Marcotte](https://ethanmarcotte.com/)** (Ethan Marcotte; blog; free). The person who named responsive design, on the web, design systems and the profession. *In a prompt:* "Is this layout truly responsive, or only resized at three points?"
- **[Jeremy Keith](https://adactio.com/)** (Jeremy Keith; blog; free). A daily journal on resilient web design and progressive enhancement. *In a prompt:* "What still works here if scripts fail to load?"
- **[DESK Magazine](https://vanschneider.com/blog/)** (Tobias van Schneider; blog; free). Articles and interviews on design craft, portfolios and working as a designer. *In a prompt:* "How would I present this work in a portfolio?"
- **[Bring the Donuts](https://www.bringthedonuts.com/)** (Ken Norton; blog; free). Essays on product management and leadership from a long-time Google product lead. *In a prompt:* "What would a great product manager want from the designer here?"
