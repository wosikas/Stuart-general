# Design resources - Visual craft

Part of [[11 Resource library]] in the [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

Type, colour, spacing and icons: the rules, and the tools that apply them.

38 links. A star marks an essential. Last link check: 18 November 2026.

- **[Visual design rules you can safely follow every time](https://anthonyhobday.com/sideprojects/saferules/)** ★ (Anthony Hobday; article; free). About thirty concrete rules, each with a picture: near-black not black, spacing in multiples, nested corner radii. Perfect for turning into prompt constraints. *In a prompt:* "Follow Anthony Hobday's safe rules; list any you chose to break."
- **[Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/)** ★ (Matthew Butterick; book; free). Read 'Typography in ten minutes' and 'Summary of key rules'. They cover line length, line spacing and font choice, which is most of what matters. *In a prompt:* "Keep body text to 45 to 90 characters per line with line spacing of 120 to 145%."
- **[Color in UI Design: A (Practical) Framework](https://www.learnui.design/blog/color-in-ui-design-a-practical-framework.html)** (Erik Kennedy; article; free). How to build a whole interface from one hue by varying brightness and saturation. *In a prompt:* "Derive all UI colours from one brand hue plus neutrals, with darker and lighter variations."
- **[Google Fonts Knowledge](https://fonts.google.com/knowledge)** (Google; reference; free). Clear lessons on choosing and pairing type, readability and variable fonts. *In a prompt:* "Pair one display face with one text face and justify the pairing."
- **[Typescale](https://typescale.com/)** (Jeremy Church; tool; free). Pick a base size and ratio, preview the whole scale and export it. *In a prompt:* "Use a 1.25 ratio type scale from a 16 px base, five steps."
- **[Utopia](https://utopia.fyi/)** (James Gilyead & Trys Mudford; tool; free). Fluid type and spacing scales that grow smoothly with the screen, with no breakpoints. *In a prompt:* "Use fluid type with clamp() between 375 and 1280 px viewports."
- **[Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/)** (Indian Type Foundry; tool; free). High-quality typefaces that are free for commercial use. An alternative to the over-used defaults. *In a prompt:* "Suggest two free typefaces that feel calm and literary, not techy."
- **[Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/)** (Fonts In Use; gallery; free). Real-world typography, indexed by typeface and industry. *In a prompt:* "Show how this typeface is used in editorial products."
- **[Material 3: Color system](https://m3.material.io/styles/color/system/overview)** (Google; guideline; free). How colour roles work: primary, surface, container and the 'on' colours that sit on top of them. *In a prompt:* "Define colour roles as primary, on-primary, surface and on-surface."
- **[Radix Colors](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors)** (WorkOS; tool; free). Twelve-step colour scales where each step has a defined job, with matched dark-mode scales. *In a prompt:* "Use a 12-step scale: steps 1–2 backgrounds, 6–8 borders, 11–12 text."
- **[Realtime Colors](https://www.realtimecolors.com/)** (Juxtopposed; tool; free). Try a palette and font pairing on a realistic page layout instantly. *In a prompt:* "Propose text, background, primary, secondary and accent colours I can preview."
- **[Leonardo](https://leonardocolor.io/)** (Adobe; tool; free). Generate colour scales by target contrast ratio, so accessibility is built into the palette. *In a prompt:* "Generate a palette where every step is defined by its contrast against the background."
- **[OKLCH Color Picker](https://oklch.com/)** (Evil Martians; tool; free). A picker for the OKLCH colour space, where equal lightness numbers look equally light. *In a prompt:* "Define the palette in OKLCH so lightness steps are perceptually even."
- **[Coolors](https://coolors.co/)** (Coolors; tool; free tier). A fast palette generator with contrast checking and colour-blindness preview. *In a prompt:* "Give me three palette options, each with a neutral scale and one accent."
- **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev/)** (Lucide; kit; free). A clean, consistent open-source icon set. The default in shadcn/ui. *In a prompt:* "Use Lucide icons at 20 px with a 1.5 px stroke throughout."
- **[Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com/)** (Phosphor; kit; free). A flexible icon family in six weights, including duotone and fill. *In a prompt:* "Use Phosphor regular for default and fill for the selected tab."
- **[Material Symbols](https://fonts.google.com/icons)** (Google; kit; free). Google's variable icon set, with adjustable weight, fill and optical size. *In a prompt:* "Use Material Symbols rounded, weight 400, filled when active."
- **[SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/)** (Apple; kit; free). Apple's icon library, designed to align with the system font. Required knowledge for native iOS. *In a prompt:* "Use SF Symbols names for every icon so they match Dynamic Type."
- **[Heroicons](https://heroicons.com/)** (Tailwind Labs; kit; free). Outline and solid icons from the makers of Tailwind. *In a prompt:* "Use Heroicons outline at 24 px for navigation."
- **[Every Layout](https://every-layout.dev/)** (Heydon Pickering & Andy Bell; reference; free tier). A small set of composable layout patterns that adapt without breakpoints: stack, cluster, sidebar, switcher. *In a prompt:* "Compose this page from stack, cluster and sidebar layout primitives."
- **[Space in Design Systems](https://medium.com/eightshapes-llc/space-in-design-systems-188bcbae0d62)** (Nathan Curtis · EightShapes; article; free). The classic article on naming and applying spacing: inset, stack and inline. *In a prompt:* "Define inset, stack and inline spacing tokens and use only those."
- **[From Data to Viz](https://www.data-to-viz.com/)** (Yan Holtz & Conor Healy; reference; free). A decision tree for choosing the right chart from the shape of your data. *In a prompt:* "Which chart type fits this data, and which common mistake should we avoid?"
- **[Building Your Color Palette](https://www.refactoringui.com/previews/building-your-color-palette)** ★ (Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger; article; free). A free chapter of Refactoring UI. Explains why you need eight to ten shades of grey, a primary colour and several accents, each in nine steps. *In a prompt:* "Define greys, one primary and three accent colours, each as a nine-step scale from 100 to 900."
- **[Typography in ten minutes](https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html)** (Matthew Butterick; article; free). Five rules that fix most typography: body text first, point size, line spacing, line length, font choice. *In a prompt:* "Set the body text first: size, line spacing of 120 to 145%, and 45 to 90 characters per line."
- **[The Elements of Typographic Style Applied to the Web](http://webtypography.net/)** (Richard Rutter; reference; free). The classic typography manual translated into practical web rules. *In a prompt:* "Apply a consistent vertical rhythm based on the body line height."
- **[5 Principles of Visual Design in UX](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/)** (NN/g; article; free). Scale, visual hierarchy, balance, contrast and Gestalt, each with interface examples. *In a prompt:* "Assess this screen on scale, hierarchy, balance, contrast and grouping."
- **[Visual Hierarchy in UX: Definition](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/)** (NN/g; article; free). How colour, contrast, scale and grouping steer the eye, with before-and-after examples. *In a prompt:* "Use no more than three sizes and two weights to set the hierarchy."
- **[Proximity Principle in Visual Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/gestalt-proximity/)** (NN/g; article; free). Things placed close together are seen as related. The cheapest way to organise a screen. *In a prompt:* "Group related items with spacing alone; remove borders and dividers where spacing does the job."
- **[Visual Design in UX: Study Guide](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-design-in-ux-study-guide/)** (NN/g; reference; free). An index of NN/g's visual design articles and videos, grouped by topic. *In a prompt:* "Give me a reading order for learning visual design fundamentals."
- **[Spacing, grids, and layouts](https://www.designsystems.com/space-grids-and-layouts/)** (DesignSystems.com · Figma; article; free). A thorough guide to spacing scales, grid types and layout rules in a design system. *In a prompt:* "Define a spacing scale, a column grid and the margins at each breakpoint."
- **[Building Better UI Designs With Layout Grids](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2017/12/building-better-ui-designs-layout-grids/)** (Nick Babich · Smashing Magazine; article; free). What columns, gutters and margins are, and how to use a grid without becoming its prisoner. *In a prompt:* "Use a 12-column grid on desktop, 8 on tablet and 4 on mobile."
- **[Designing Beautiful Shadows in CSS](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/designing-shadows/)** (Josh W. Comeau; article; free). How to make shadows look real: one light source, layered shadows and colour-matched tones. *In a prompt:* "Use layered shadows from one consistent light source, tinted to match the background."
- **[Color: From Hexcodes to Eyeballs](https://jamie-wong.com/post/color/)** (Jamie Wong; article; free). A deep, clear explanation of how colour actually works, from light to screens to perception. *In a prompt:* "Explain why these two colours with the same lightness value look different in brightness."
- **[Color and Contrast](https://colorandcontrast.com/)** (Nate Baldwin; reference; free). An interactive guide to colour theory and contrast for interface designers. *In a prompt:* "Explain which contrast effect makes this grey look different on the two backgrounds."
- **[A Complete Guide to CSS Flexbox](https://css-tricks.com/snippets/css/a-guide-to-flexbox/)** (CSS-Tricks; reference; free). The illustrated reference for one-dimensional layout. Learn its words, such as justify, align, wrap and gap, to describe layouts precisely. *In a prompt:* "Lay this row out with flexbox: space-between, centre aligned, wrapping on small screens."
- **[A Complete Guide to CSS Grid Layout](https://css-tricks.com/complete-guide-css-grid-layout/)** (CSS-Tricks; reference; free). The illustrated reference for two-dimensional layout: tracks, areas and auto-fit. *In a prompt:* "Use a grid with auto-fit columns of at least 280 px."
- **[Typewolf](https://www.typewolf.com/)** (Jeremiah Shoaf; gallery; free tier). What typefaces real sites are using, with pairing suggestions and free alternatives. *In a prompt:* "Suggest a pairing in the spirit of these popular typefaces, using free fonts."
- **[Pimp my Type](https://pimpmytype.com/)** (Oliver Schöndorfer; blog; free). Practical typography for interface designers, in articles and short videos. *In a prompt:* "Fix the typography on this screen and explain each change."
