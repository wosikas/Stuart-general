# Design resources - Top design websites

Part of [[11 Resource library]] in the [[Product Design Prompt Kit]].

Publications, company design blogs, newsletters and directories worth bookmarking.

37 links. A star marks an essential. Last link check: 18 November 2026.

- **[A List Apart](https://alistapart.com/)** ★ (A List Apart; website; free). The magazine where responsive design, mobile-first forms and much of modern web practice were first argued in print. Deep, edited, lasting articles. *In a prompt:* "What has A List Apart published on this topic, and what is the core argument?"
- **[Google Design](https://design.google/)** ★ (Google; website; free). Case studies, interviews and resources from Google's design teams, including the thinking behind Material. *In a prompt:* "How did Google's team approach a problem like this one?"
- **[Apple Design](https://developer.apple.com/design/)** (Apple; website; free). The front door to Apple's guidelines, design videos, awards and downloadable resources. *In a prompt:* "Which Apple Design Award winners solve a similar problem, and how?"
- **[Microsoft Design](https://microsoft.design/)** (Microsoft; website; free). Articles on Fluent, inclusive design and designing AI features into everyday products. *In a prompt:* "How does Microsoft's team introduce an AI feature into an existing product?"
- **[Adobe Design](https://adobe.design/)** (Adobe; website; free). Stories and process write-ups from the teams behind Adobe's creative tools. *In a prompt:* "How do teams building complex creative tools keep them learnable?"
- **[Slack Design](https://slack.design/)** (Slack; website; free). Essays from Slack's designers on craft, content and collaboration. *In a prompt:* "Write this the way Slack's product copy would: clear, concise, human."
- **[IBM Design](https://www.ibm.com/design/)** (IBM; website; free). IBM's design philosophy, including its enterprise design thinking framework. *In a prompt:* "Run a quick 'hills' exercise: who, what, and the wow for this release."
- **[Linear: Now](https://linear.app/now)** (Linear; blog; free). Essays from the Linear team on quality, craft and how they build. *In a prompt:* "What would a team obsessed with quality refuse to ship in this design?"
- **[Notion Blog](https://www.notion.com/blog)** (Notion; blog; free). Product and design writing from a direct neighbour of Loom, including how its building blocks work. *In a prompt:* "How does Notion explain this concept to new users?"
- **[Stripe Blog](https://stripe.com/blog)** (Stripe; blog; free). Occasional but excellent posts on design systems, colour and front-end craft. *In a prompt:* "What level of detail would Stripe apply to this interaction?"
- **[Vercel Blog](https://vercel.com/blog)** (Vercel; blog; free). Design engineering write-ups from the team behind v0 and the Geist design system. *In a prompt:* "Describe the design engineering details that make this feel fast."
- **[Figma: Design systems](https://www.designsystems.com/)** (Figma; website; free). Figma's publication on design systems: foundations, case studies and practical guides. *In a prompt:* "What is the smallest design system that would serve this product?"
- **[Framer Blog](https://www.framer.com/blog/)** (Framer; blog; free). Web design, layout and motion articles from a tool built around them. *In a prompt:* "What current web layout and motion conventions should this marketing page follow?"
- **[Webflow Blog](https://webflow.com/blog)** (Webflow; blog; free). A large library of web design tutorials, trend round-ups and examples. *In a prompt:* "What are the standard sections of a good product landing page?"
- **[Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/)** (Duolingo; blog; free). Posts on experiments, habit design and product decisions from a company that tests everything. *In a prompt:* "What experiment would tell us whether this change improves retention?"
- **[Intercom Blog](https://www.intercom.com/blog/)** (Intercom; blog; free). Years of clear writing on product strategy, design principles and jobs-to-be-done. *In a prompt:* "State the principle behind this decision in one sentence."
- **[UX Magazine](https://uxmag.com/)** (UX Magazine; website; free). A long-running publication, now focused heavily on designing with and for AI. *In a prompt:* "What are practitioners saying about designing AI agents into products?"
- **[UXmatters](https://www.uxmatters.com/)** (UXmatters; website; free). Practitioner articles and columns on research, strategy and mobile design since 2005. *In a prompt:* "What does field research say about this behaviour?"
- **[Boxes and Arrows](https://boxesandarrows.com/)** (Boxes and Arrows; website; free). The original journal of information architecture and interaction design. *In a prompt:* "How would an information architect organise and label this content?"
- **[UX Movement](https://uxmovement.com/)** (Anthony Tseng; website; free). Short, illustrated articles that each settle one interface question, such as button order or label position. *In a prompt:* "Which goes first here, the primary or the secondary button, and why?"
- **[UX Myths](https://uxmyths.com/)** (Zoltán Gócza & Zoltán Kollin; reference; free). Common design misconceptions, each debunked with research: the three-click rule, 'people don't scroll', and more. *In a prompt:* "Is this decision resting on a UX myth? Check it against the evidence."
- **[Case Study Club](https://www.casestudy.club/)** (Case Study Club; website; free). A curated collection of detailed design case studies, showing process as well as outcome. *In a prompt:* "Write this up as a case study: problem, constraints, options, decision, result."
- **[CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/)** (CSS-Tricks; website; free). The reference for how layouts are actually built. Useful for learning the words that describe layout to an AI. *In a prompt:* "Describe this layout in flexbox and grid terms."
- **[web.dev](https://web.dev/)** (Google; website; free). Google's guidance on building fast, accessible, well-designed web pages, with free courses. *In a prompt:* "What do Core Web Vitals require of this design?"
- **[Prototypr](https://prototypr.io/)** (Prototypr; website; free). An open publication of design stories, tools and tutorials from the community. *In a prompt:* "What new design tools are worth trying for this job?"
- **[Muzli](https://muz.li/)** (Muzli; website; free). A daily feed of design inspiration and articles pulled from across the web. *In a prompt:* "Summarise the current visual trends relevant to this kind of product."
- **[It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/)** (It's Nice That; website; free). Graphic design, illustration and branding. Good for art direction beyond interface clichés. *In a prompt:* "Suggest an art direction for this brand that doesn't look like every other app."
- **[Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/)** (AIGA; website; free). Thoughtful writing on graphic design culture, history and practice. *In a prompt:* "What design tradition could this visual identity draw on?"
- **[Mind the Product](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/)** (Mind the Product; website; free). The largest product management community: articles and talks on discovery, strategy and prioritisation. *In a prompt:* "How should we prioritise these features against each other?"
- **[First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/)** (First Round; website; free). Long, practical interviews with founders and product leaders on how they actually work. *In a prompt:* "What process did a successful team use for a decision like this?"
- **[Reforge Blog](https://www.reforge.com/blog)** (Reforge; blog; free). Frameworks for growth, retention and product strategy from experienced operators. *In a prompt:* "Map the retention loop this feature is meant to strengthen."
- **[People Nerds](https://dscout.com/people-nerds)** (dscout; blog; free). Articles and interviews for user researchers, with templates and method guides. *In a prompt:* "Give me a template for synthesising these interview notes."
- **[UX Tools](https://uxtools.co/)** (UX Tools; website; free). News and comparisons of design tools, plus the annual survey of what designers use. *In a prompt:* "Compare the main tools for this job and recommend one."
- **[Toools.design](https://www.toools.design/)** (Toools.design; reference; free). A large directory of design tools and resources, sorted by category. *In a prompt:* "What tools exist for this specific design task?"
- **[UX Design Weekly](https://uxdesignweekly.com/)** (Kenny Chen; blog; free). A weekly email of the best UX links, running for over a decade. *In a prompt:* "What were the most-shared UX articles on this topic recently?"
- **[HeyDesigner](https://heydesigner.com/)** (HeyDesigner; blog; free). Hand-picked design and front-end links, updated daily. *In a prompt:* "Find me three recent, well-regarded articles on this pattern."
- **[TLDR Design](https://tldr.tech/design)** (TLDR; blog; free). A five-minute daily email of design news, tools and articles. *In a prompt:* "What happened in design tooling this week that affects how I should work?"
