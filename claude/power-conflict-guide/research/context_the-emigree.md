## 5. Form
- Free verse: B-FV "poetry organized to the cadences of speech and image patterns rather than according to a regular metrical scheme" (verified in context_tissue.md). No regular rhyme scheme: SAFE (verify4).
- Our count from anth.txt: 25 lines; stanzas of 8, 8, 9 lines; each stanza ends with "sunlight" (lines 8, 16, 25). First person "I" throughout; past tense (line 1) moves to present.

## Gaps and conflicts
1. No statement by Rumens about the poem on an openable page apart from the Telegraph obituary's report ("forbidden relationship"). Guardian blocked.
2. First publication of the poem before 1993: GAP. Say "collected in Thinking of Skins (1993)".
3. Which country (if any) the poem is about: GAP and deliberately so; the poem names none. Bitesize/Wikipedia claims not used.
4. Prague/dissidents, Russia 1987 and Drobyshev leaving the USSR in 1978: Telegraph only.
5. Spelling: AQA anthology "The Émigree"/"CAROLE RUMENS" vs papers "The Emigrée"/"Carol Rumens"; Pearson "There was once" vs AQA "There once was". Use AQA paper title and anthology wording.
6. PMT says she "lived in Belfast and Wales"; sources say she taught most extensively in Belfast and Bangor, lived in Northern Ireland in 1993 (GB-SKINS) and later had a cottage near Bangor (TEL: "where she bought a rural cottage, dividing her time between there and Shepherd's Bush"). PMT's "collection ... centred on political consciousness in Russia and East Europe" is an overstatement; the blurb says "Often set against the background of Eastern Europe, Russia or her present home in Northern Ireland".
