# Power and Conflict: AQA-backed theme tags per poem

Source: built only from `exam_materials.md`, which gives verbatim AQA question papers (QP), mark-scheme indicative content (MS) and examiners' reports (ER). The four reports (Nov 2020, Nov 2021 1P, June 2022 1P, June 2023) were re-checked against the downloaded report PDFs' extracted text. No further poem mentions were found beyond those listed in exam_materials.md.

**Rule applied.** A poem gets a theme only in two cases:
- (a) An AQA question names the poem with that focus.
- (b) An MS or ER bullet names the poem in connection with that idea. When a bullet lists a poem as a partner, the tag follows the bullet's own idea. A bullet that names only a method (such as "first person", "brutal imagery" or "tone") gives no theme tag.

Text in quotation marks is copied verbatim from AQA. Rows marked **(indirect)** fall outside the strict rule: the bullet sits in that poem's own question but does not name the poem. They are kept for transparency, flagged, and left out of the strict theme map.

**Source keys**
- **QP** = question paper.
- **SMS** = Specimen MS (AQA-87022-SMS).
- **MS17 / MS18 / MS19 / MS24 / MS25** = June mark schemes (PMT copies of AQA PDFs).
- **MS-N20** = Nov 2020 MS (June 2020 paper).
- **MS-N21** = Nov 2021 8702/1P MS.
- **MS-J22** = June 2022 8702/1P MS.
- **MS23** = June 2023 MS.
- **ER-N20, ER-N21, ER-J22, ER23** = the examiners' reports for those series.

## Gaps and conflicts
- GAP: Seven vocabulary themes have no AQA backing in any public QP, MS or ER: Memory, Loss, Guilt, Fear, Isolation, Art outlasts power and Childhood. Pride has indirect backing only. If the guide uses these themes, it must label them as the guide's own readings, not AQA links.
- GAP: Examiners' reports for 2017, 2018, 2019, 2024 and 2025 are not public, so tags from those years rest on the mark schemes only.
- MAPPING CHOICES TO REVIEW:
  - MS17 "the relationship between the individual soldiers and those for whom they fought" is mapped to Patriotism and duty for Charge of the Light Brigade and Exposure.
  - "imagery of war in ‘Storm on the Island’" is mapped to War, flagged as imagery only.
  - The "sand" bullet (MS18) is attached to Ozymandias, flagged as indirect.
  - SMS "Use of speaker" is mapped to Power for Tissue and CHOMH, as weak.
- AQA CAUTIONS: ER-N21 says students "struggled" with Storm on the Island and Poppies for the power-and-control question. ER-J22 calls Storm, CHOMH, London and Ozymandias "arguably a little more challenging" for "ideas about conflict". Ozymandias is therefore **not** tagged Conflict.
- TITLE SPELLINGS vary in AQA documents ("The Émigré", "The Émigrée", "The Emigrée", "The émigree"; "Checkin’ Out Me History" in MS23). They are kept verbatim inside quotes.
