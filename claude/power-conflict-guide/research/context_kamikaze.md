## Gaps and conflicts
1. Garland's birth year/place (1938, Surrey) and NPC year (2001): WJEC only; poet's site 503, Poetry Society 403.
2. No reliable author statement about why she wrote Kamikaze (GAP); WJEC's one-line quote is second-hand.
3. Kamikaze itself: no evidence it won a prize. The collection was Forward-shortlisted (2014) and won the Straid award (2013, publisher's own claim).
4. Publication year: 2013 (Templar list, WJEC); PMT's 2014 is wrong.
5. WJEC's Pearl Harbor claim: not supported (kamikaze attacks date from October 1944). Don't use.
6. Ships sunk: Britannica 34 (whole war); an IWM figure (47) seen only in a search snippet (page 403). Pilots killed: Guardian "more than 3 800", WJEC "over 3,000".
7. Shame of returning: one pilot's testimony (Guardian 2015); nothing reliable on families shunning returners (GAP).
