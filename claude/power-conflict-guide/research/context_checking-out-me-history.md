## 5. Form (our count from anth.txt, plus sources)

- 53 lines (counting non-blank lines in anth.txt, ignoring the page number). No full stops or commas anywhere; one apostrophe only (L'Ouverture). Our check.
- Two kinds of section: unindented "Dem tell me" stanzas (long lines, rhyming couplets/triplets: cat/dat; balloon/moon/spoon/maroon; Waterloo/Zulu/1492/too; lamp/camp; soul/Seacole) and indented hero sections of very short lines (Toussaint 10–21, Nanny 26–31, Seacole 40–49). Our check.
- The AQA anthology PDF uses an italic font (Arial,Italic) on the pages with the hero sections (pdffonts check on pages 39–40). PMT says the hero sections are in italics. Status: our check + PMT lead; describe as "set apart (indented, and in italics in the anthology)".
- Dialect / Creole spellings: "dem", "dat", "de", "me" for "my", missing verb endings ("she travel", "I checking"). Sources for dialect: PA, CPA-POEM ("Dialect").
