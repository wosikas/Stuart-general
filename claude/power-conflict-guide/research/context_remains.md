## 7. PMT errors / leads checked
- PMT heads the poem page "MOTHER ANY DISTANCE" (a different Armitage poem): a slip.
- PMT: "The poem and documentary coincided with changing public opinion ... Iraq war was condemned as unnecessary, and potentially due to US oil greed": NOT verified in any allowed source; do not use.
- PMT: The Not Dead (2007) film: matches. "Collection was based on Guardsman Tromans": overclaims (the collection covers three veterans; Remains alone is linked to the Iraq soldier).
- PMT: "Body being treated like an object", "tosses": a reading, consistent with WJEC.
