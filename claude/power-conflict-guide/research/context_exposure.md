## 7. Text notes

- AQA anthology (master): "knive us", "fingering stealth", "Now ever suns", "grey", "Tonight, His frost", "their shaking grasp". Line 37 prints "Shrivelling many hands. puckering foreheads crisp." (full stop, apparently a misprint; don't quote it).
- The June 2025 question paper printed "Nor ever suns", "Tonight, this frost", "Shrivelling many hands," and "picks and shovels in shaking grasp" (exam_materials.md). So the printed exam text can differ slightly from the anthology: quote the paper in front of you.

## Gaps and conflicts
- Drafting date: four different claims (Abbeville Feb 1917 begun; Feb 1918 written; Sept 1918 final; Oxford Dec 1917 begun — blocked). Present as a conflict.
- Shell blast: April (PA) vs May 1917 (OX).
- Britannica's "Lancashire Fusiliers" conflicts with the Manchester Regiment (OX, POETS, WOA); use Manchester Regiment.
- Preface date May 1918 is single source (Durham via Google Arts & Culture); WOA says written in Ripon in 1918.
- PMT's claims that Owen thought the church "hypocritical", that a therapist told him to write, and that "the poem was written in 1917 whilst Owen was fighting in the trenches" are NOT verified (GAP / contradicted by the dating sources).
- Blocked: BL blog, IWM pages, Oxford WW1 archive, Napier, 1914-1918-online.
