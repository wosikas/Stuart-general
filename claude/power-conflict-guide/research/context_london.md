## PMT notes checked

- "anti-monarchy": POETS "opposition to the English monarchy". PMT's "wanted a revolution to remove it, thinking that revolution was inevitable": NOT found in an allowed source. Do not use.
- "London was published during the reign of terror": NOT verified (no allowed source opened gives Reign of Terror dates). Do not use.

## Gaps and conflicts
1. Publication year conflict: 1794 (Britannica, poets.org) vs 1793 (Tate "published"; Cash "completed"). Use 1794.
2. Paine passage date: Cash says 1791, but the passage is in *Rights of Man* Part II, dated Feb 1792 (Gutenberg text). Use 1792.
3. Draft date 1792: British Library only. Draft revisions ("dirty", "German-forged"): Cash only.
4. Blake–Paine acquaintance: poets.org only among allowed sources (Britannica Paine page truncated).
5. GAP: no allowed source opened confirms the soldier-blood/Hampton Court slogans (Cash says "may"); not used.
6. GAP: no AQA warning specific to London misreadings; only the general ER23 warning.
