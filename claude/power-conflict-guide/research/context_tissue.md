## 5. Form (our count from anth.txt + definitions)
- 37 lines: nine four-line stanzas (quatrains) + a final one-line stanza ("turned into your skin."). Our count. One stanza break (after line 32) falls at a page break in the anthology; it fits the four-line pattern.
- No regular metre and no regular rhyme scheme (our check; a few chimes, e.g. "break"/"make", lines 29 and 31). So it is free verse: B-FV "poetry organized to the cadences of speech and image patterns rather than according to a regular metrical scheme." POETS-FV: "poetry not dictated by an established form or meter and often influenced by the rhythms of speech." Definition SAFE; applying it = our check.
- Heavy enjambment, including across stanza breaks (lines 20–21, 28–29, 32–33). B-ENJ: "the continuation of the sense of a phrase beyond the end of a line of verse."
- Lines 25–37 are one long sentence (our count).
