## KEY NEW FINDING: AQA's own anthology text (resolves most text conflicts)

**Source:** AQA, *GCSE English Literature (8702) Past and present: poetry anthology*, "SAMPLE", "For examinations from 2017", Version 1.2 (PDF metadata: "AQA Anthology of Poetry: Poems past and present", created 15 Jul 2015, 44 pp).
URL: https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/english/AQA-8702-TG-POEMS.PDF (opened; downloaded directly from AQA filestore, HTTP 200).
- It prints the full texts of all three poems (pp. 22, 27–28, 29).
- Its Acknowledgements say: "Seamus Heaney 'Storm on the Island' from Opened Ground by permission of Faber and Faber Ltd." So this is a Faber text reproduced by the exam board, which makes it the best second source available.
- Caveat: it is labelled SAMPLE, version 1.2 (2015). The student's printed anthology is very probably the same, but a later reprint could differ in tiny details. Contents title in this PDF reads "Extract from, The Prelude" (with a comma); the question papers print "Extract from The Prelude".

### Results of checking against it
- **Storm on the Island:** the AQA text matches the PMT text word for word in every line the strand quotes. It is 19 lines in one block. It reads **"We are bombarded by the empty air."** This settles the "by"/"with" question: use **"by"**. ("with" came from an unopened allpoetry snippet and is not supported.) The only difference from PMT that I found: PMT puts a full stop after "Exploding comfortably down on the cliffs"; AQA has no punctuation there. None of the strand's quotations is affected.
- **The Prelude:** AQA prints **"Within a rocky cove"**, NOT the 1850/Knight "rocky cave". So the student's anthology almost certainly says "cove". The 44-line span is the same ("One summer evening (led by her) I found" to "By day, and were a trouble to my dreams."). Small punctuation differences from Knight: AQA has "instinct," (with comma) and "my bark, –".
- **Exposure:** AQA's text is the modern text with one exception:
  - "knive us": AQA agrees with poets.org.
  - "fingering stealth": AQA agrees with poets.org.
  - "Now ever suns": AQA agrees with poets.org.
  - "grey": AQA agrees with poets.org.
  - **"Tonight, His frost"**: AQA has "His" (as in 1920), NOT poets.org's "this". It is spelled "Tonight", not "To-night".
  - AQA also prints "Low, drooping flares" and "– Is it that we are dying?".
  - AQA's line "Shrivelling many hands. puckering foreheads crisp." has a full stop that looks like a typo. Don't quote that line.

## USE WITH CARE

1. **Storm is in *Death of a Naturalist*:** still only PMT on an opened page. AQA's acknowledgement credits the text "from Opened Ground" (Heaney's selected poems), which neither confirms nor contradicts it. The estate page, Britannica, NYRB and the Irish Times don't name the poem. Keep it as SINGLE SOURCE. It is very probably right, but no reliable page I opened says so.
2. **The "three Aran poems" and the Aran Islands setting:** PMT only (revision site). Keep it as "PMT says…".
3. **Britannica on Heaney's upbringing:** the strand paraphrases it as "grew up Catholic in a mainly Protestant area". Britannica's wording is "Raised Roman Catholic in the predominantly Protestant North". Use Britannica's wording: it says "the North", not "area".
4. **Prelude "volta" at "Upreared":** "volta" is properly a sonnet term. For an examiner it is safer to call this the turning point or shift. The reading itself is fine.
5. **Exposure text conflict:** this is now resolved for the student, but the guide should say plainly that he should quote **AQA's** wording: "knive us", "fingering", "Now ever suns", "grey", "Tonight, His frost". The strand's suggestion to check "His/this" is answered: **His**.
6. **Exposure date of writing:** the conflict is still real. Poetry Archive says "written in February 1918"; Williams says "final version… September 1918". Both remain single-source claims. Keep it presented as a conflict.
7. **The Gutenberg Owen text** is a transcript of the **1921 reprint** of the 1920 edition. Its note reads: "The 1920 edition was the first edition of Owen's poems, the 1921 reprint (of which this is a transcript) added one more". Call it "1920 text (1921 reprint)".
8. **Poets.org text of Exposure** differs from AQA at "this frost". poets.org also has "shaking grasp" where AQA and 1920 have "their shaking grasp", and it has no stanza numbering. Don't treat poets.org as the anthology text.

## DROP / FIX

1. **Storm "CONFLICT: bombarded by / bombarded with"** (strand quotation list and Gaps item 1). FIX: there is no conflict in any reliable source. The AQA anthology (Faber text) reads "We are bombarded by the empty air." Drop the "with" variant.
2. **"Every quotation below is SINGLE SOURCE / UNVERIFIED against the anthology"** (Storm). FIX: all 8 quotations are verified against the AQA anthology PDF (URL above). Relabel them as verified by PMT and AQA.
3. **Prelude "rocky cave / rocky cove".** FIX: AQA's anthology prints "**Within a rocky cove**". Tell the student his anthology says "cove". The 1850 first edition says "cave", which can be mentioned as an AO3 curiosity only.
4. **Exposure "1920 date is SINGLE SOURCE… not confirmed on an opened page".** FIX: the Gutenberg file itself (opened) says "The 1920 edition was the first edition of Owen's poems". Poetry Archive says the poems were "mostly published after his death". The 1920 date can stand.
5. **Owen letter "(4 February 1917) about freezing in the open"** (Exposure AO3). FIX: wrong date attribution. In Ford's LRB review the "feet ached" sentence is from "The letter recounting their time on Redan Ridge". The episode is dated "At the end of January, he and his platoon spent two days and nights without shelter in a snowstorm on Redan Ridge… It was so bitter their water cans froze, so they had nothing to drink." The **4 February** letter in the review is a different letter, to his mother Susan, beginning "Hideous landscapes, vile noises, foul language…". Correct the attribution to: Redan Ridge, end of January 1917, letter home (date not given in the review). The LRB also gives the linking sentence the strand lacked: "'Exposure' presents an unrelenting account of their desperate vigil". That sentence can be added.
6. **Gaps list item 4: "I could not open an AQA page giving the version or line range"**. FIX: the AQA sample anthology PDF gives the exact text and span (= Knight Book I, 357–400, with "cove").
7. **Exposure "Check 'His/this frost' and 'fingering/lingering' against the anthology"**. FIX: answered. AQA has "Tonight, His frost" and "fingering stealth".
