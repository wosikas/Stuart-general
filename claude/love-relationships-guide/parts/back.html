GGlossary: the terms in this guide

You only need a term if it helps you explain an effect. Examiners reward the effect, not the label.
TermWhat it meansExample from the poems
SimileA comparison using “like” or “as”“my thoughts do twine and bud / About thee, as wild vines, about a tree” (Sonnet 29)
Metaphor / extended metaphorDescribing one thing as if it were another; “extended” when it carries on through the poemthe vines and the tree in Sonnet 29
PersonificationGiving something non-human human actions or feelings“The fountains mingle with the river” (Love's Philosophy)
ParadoxA statement that seems to contradict itself but makes a point“the deadest thing / Alive enough to have strength to die” (Neutral Tones)
RepetitionRepeating a word for effect“That moment she was mine, mine, fair,” (Porphyria’s Lover)
EnjambmentA sentence running on over a line break“and all her hair / In one long yellow string I wound” (Porphyria’s Lover)
CaesuraA pause or full stop in the middle of a line“I think of thee! – my thoughts do twine and bud” (Sonnet 29)
DialectThe way a language is spoken in a particular place or community“Hey Singh, ver yoo bin?” (Singh Song!)
Pathetic fallacyWeather or nature that reflects a character's feelingsthe cold, grey winter scene in Neutral Tones
VoltaThe “turn” in a poem, where the thought changes directionthe turn in Sonnet 29, which AQA's examiners discussed in 2022
SonnetA 14-line poem, usually in iambic pentameter with a set rhyme patternSonnet 29
Dramatic monologueA poem spoken by one character who is not the poet, often to a silent listenerPorphyria’s Lover; The Farmer’s Bride
Free versePoetry with no regular rhyme or rhythmsee the form section on each poem page
First / second person“I” and “we” (first person); “you” (second person)“you” addressed throughout Before You Were Mine
Romantic poetsA group of poets around 1800, including Shelley and Byron. Britannica says Wordsworth and Coleridge's Lyrical Ballads (1798) “helped launch the English Romantic movement”.Love's Philosophy, When We Two Parted
