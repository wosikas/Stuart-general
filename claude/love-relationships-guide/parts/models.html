PRACTICE

Model answers

Three full answers to real AQA questions, covering all four anchor poems and both kinds of question: romantic love and family. Beside every paragraph, a panel explains exactly what earns the marks, using AQA's own words.

These answers were written to AQA's top-level (Level 6) descriptors. They have not been marked by AQA, and nobody outside AQA can guarantee a mark.

★AQA's own example answers

AQA has not published an example answer for a Love and Relationships question. Its one published example for this part of the paper is on the Power and Conflict cluster. Its examiner's comment shows what top answers do: “The student holds both poems together which enables a considered comparison to be made… By the end, this comparison becomes evaluative as there is a judgement made as to which poem demonstrates the effects of power more strongly.” AQA, GCSE English Literature Assessment guide (AQA-8702-EX-COMMENTARY)

What to copy: answer the question for both poems in your first sentence; keep both poems in play; end with a judgement about which poem shows the idea more strongly, and why.
