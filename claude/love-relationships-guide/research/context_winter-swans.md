## 4. Form (our count from ANTH; style backing from CREW/PA)
- 20 lines: six tercets (3-line stanzas) and a final couplet. Our count.
- No regular rhyme scheme or metre. Sound echoes: "water" (l.10) / "weather" (l.12) half rhyme; "light" (l.15) / "flight" (l.20) full rhyme across stanzas; the final couplet itself does not rhyme ("other" / "flight"). Our check.
- Enjambment across stanzas: e.g. ll.3–4, 6–7, 9–10, 15–16, 18–19 run on. Our check.
- Speaker "I", addressee "you" (direct speech l.13); "we"/"our" collective pronouns throughout. Our check.
