## 5. Form (our count from AQA-ANTH)

- 20 lines: four quatrains (lines 1–16), a three-line stanza (17–19) and a final single line (20). NOTE: PMT and a search snippet say five four-line stanzas; that is wrong for AQA's text.
- Rhyme: ABAB-type pattern of mostly half-rhymes: Rock/Jack, suit/feet, dress/grass, hat/light, straight/out, screw/blue (full), suns/spins, way/Leisurely, bank/think, is!/this. Line 20 rhymes (half) with line 18.
- Lines are of similar length, about ten syllables (line 2 = 10; line 1 = 13): loose, not strict metre. PMT's "iambic pentameter" claim not used.
- Present tense throughout until line 20 ("I had not thought": past perfect).
- Ballad link: PA says Causley drew "above all" on ballads; no source calls this poem a ballad. GAP.
