## 4. Form (own count from anth.txt, plus sources)
- Two stanzas of 8 lines; rhyme ABABCDCD in each. UCM: "Love's philosophy is divided in two 8-line stanzas with an ABABCDCD rhyme scheme." Our count agrees (river/ever, Ocean/emotion, single/mingle, divine/thine; Heaven/forgiven, another/brother, earth/worth, sea/me).
- Each stanza ends with a question (lines 8 and 16). Own count.
- Hunt called it a "lyric" and a "song" (IND). Metre: PMT calls it trochaic; NOT verified — avoid naming the metre; describe it as short, song-like lines alternating longer and shorter (own observation).

## 5. AQA material for this poem (from lr/exam_materials_lr.md)
- Never printed. Named as a partner in MS19, MS-N21, MS-J22, MS24; among the most popular partners in ER-J22 ("All of these worked well").
- MS19 (Winter Swans Q): "possible comparison of the hope in ‘Winter Swans’ with the less hopeful ‘When We Two Parted’ or ‘Love’s Philosophy’"; "the contrast between the start and end of ‘Winter Swans’ with ‘Love’s Philosophy’".
- MS-N21: "possible contrast between negative and positive effects of strong feelings, eg ‘Love’s Philosophy’ or ‘Sonnet 29’".
- MS-J22 (Sonnet 29 Q): "possible connections between imagery used to present ideas about the power of love, such as ‘Love’s Philosophy’".
- MS24: "valid treatment of negative types of powerful feelings, eg in ‘Winter Swans’ or ‘Love’s Philosophy’"; "direct address in ‘Neutral Tones’ compared with ‘Winter Swans’ or ‘Love’s Philosophy’".
- ER-J22 warning on "the ABAB of Love’s Philosophy" (descriptive structure comments limit marks).

## 6. PMT notes checked
- **Wrong:** the Darwin link ("questioning of the nature of religion at the time caused by the theories of Charles Darwin"). Darwin's *Origin of Species* is 1859, decades after 1819 — do not use (dates not researched further; simply dropped).
- **Wrong text:** PMT's annotated poem prints "What is all this sweet work worth," — the Hutchinson/Stacey version, not AQA's "What are all these kisses worth," (1.6). Its analysis of "sweet work worth" therefore does not fit the exam text.
- **Unverified:** "attained a small noble rank in 1806" (not checked; not used); "trochaic metre … ballad metre" (not verified; not used); "speak from his own experience" (no source; not used).
- **Verified:** ABABCDCD; two 8-line stanzas; Eton and Oxford; expulsion over atheism pamphlet (with the correction in context_shelley 7.2).

## Gaps and conflicts
- GAP: exact date/place of composition (only: published 22 Dec 1819; Shelley in Florence autumn 1819).
- GAP: whom the poem was written for. Stacey received a copy; no source says it was written for her.
- CONFLICT: Stacey pocket-book inscription dated "Decbr 29th 1820" (Eton, Hutchinson) vs Maidstone placing her with the Shelleys in Florence from Nov 1819 for two months. Not resolved; avoid the date.
- Romanticism definition remains Britannica-only.
