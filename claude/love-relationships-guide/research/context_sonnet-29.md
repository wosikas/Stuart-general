## 5. Form (our count from ANTH + pc/sonnet.md)
- 14 lines, one block. Rhyme: bud/wood/understood/should (A), tree/see/thee/instantly (B) = **ABBAABBA**; bare/everywhere/air (C), insphere thee/hear thee/near thee (D) = **CDCDCD**. This is a textbook Petrarchan rhyme scheme (pc/sonnet.md: octave "abbaabba", sestet may be "cdcdcd" — B-S, POETS, PA). SAFE (our count + definitions).
- The D rhymes are two-syllable "feminine" endings with "thee" repeated (insphere thee / hear thee / near thee). Our observation.
- Volta: pc/sonnet.md: the turn is "often indicated by such initial words as But, Yet, or And yet" (B-V) and traditionally comes between lines 8 and 9 (POETS, B-V, PA). In Sonnet 29 "Yet" opens line 5. AQA ER-J22: students wrote about "the unusual placing of the volta". So: early turn at line 5 = our reading backed by the definition and by AQA's report. A second shift at line 12 ("Because") = our reading.
- The long sentence runs from line 5 across the octave/sestet break to line 11 ("everywhere!"). Our observation.
- Iambic pentameter as the norm for sonnets: pc/sonnet.md (SAFE). Line 1 scans as ten syllables (our count). No source on this poem's metre opened: GAP.
- **AQA warning (ER-J22):** "There usually followed some comment on the ABBA rhyme scheme ... There is a risk of comments on poetic construction becoming rather descriptive".
