## 4. AQA on this poem (from lr/exam_materials_lr.md, verbatim)
- Never printed. Named only in the specimen mark scheme (Follower, "attitudes towards a parent"): AO1 "Any valid comparisons between speaker in ‘Follower’ and speaker in: ‘Mother, Any Distance’, ‘Before You Were Mine’, ‘Letters From Yorkshire’ or ‘Eden Rock’"; AO2 "Comparison of use of imagery of nature to present parent in ‘Letters From Yorkshire’".
- Check against the poem: the text says only "he", "his garden", "write to me", "You", "our souls"; line 5 "It’s not romance, simply how things are." Nothing names a parent. So: the relationship is open (a loved one far away); "parent" is AQA's reading, and the mark scheme shows the poem can be used for a family question.

## 5. Form (our count from anth.txt)
15 lines, five tercets (3-line stanzas), no rhyme scheme, no regular metre; long lines; heavy enjambment, including across stanzas (lines 3–4, 6–7, 12–13). Pronouns move he → me → You → our. (No outside source opened on form; all our check.)
