## 5. PMT checks
- PMT: "Claire Clairmont (Mary Shelley’s half sister)": WRONG per brief; not used.
- PMT: "accentual verse ... two stresses per line": GALE agrees on two stresses; "Old English" claim not used.
- PMT: "irregular line lengths": not used (lines are all short, 4–7 syllables; our count).
- PMT's "Cyclic narrative" quote pairs "silence and tears" with "rue thee, Too deeply to tell" as the ending: wrong, the poem ends "With silence and tears." Not used.
- PMT "Lady Frances Wedderburn Webster": confirmed by GALE (one source) and BRIT (flirtation only).
