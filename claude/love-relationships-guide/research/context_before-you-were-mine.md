## 5. Form (GLA + our count from ANTH)

- 20 lines, 4 stanzas × 5 lines (counted). GLA: "four stanzas that each have five lines (quintains), which gives the poem a clear and controlled shape. However, there is no strict rhyme scheme and Duffy uses a free verse form. This gives the poem a natural and conversational tone". Our count: line-end words on/Duff/holding/pavement/Marilyn — no rhyme scheme. SAFE for the count; "free verse" USE WITH CARE (one source).
- GLA: "Structurally, the poem shifts between past and present, from the time before the speaker was born, her own childhood, and the present day." Present tense for the mother's past ("you laugh on", "your Ma stands") (text).
- Title phrase repeated: line 9 and line 20 (counted). GLA: "the final repetition of the title returns the poem to its the central tension ... This creates a cyclical structure".
- Second person "you" throughout: AQA MS17 AO2: "second person in ‘Before You Were Mine’".

## 6. AQA's own angle (verbatim, from AQA files)

- June 2023 Q25: "Compare how poets present relationships between parents and children in ‘Before You Were Mine’ and in one other poem from ‘Love and relationships’."
- ER23 misreading: "A small minority of students misunderstand ‘Before You Were Mine’ and think that the speaker’s mother has died, which is a shame as it means they can miss the point of the poem."
- ER23 praised reading: "the speaker’s desire to understand the woman their mother was before she was a mother, with perhaps an undertone of envy / longing".
- ER23 partners: "‘Follower’ and ‘Mother, Any Distance’ were by far the most popular choices of second poem with also lots of choices of ‘Eden Rock’, ‘Walking Away’ and ‘Climbing My Grandfather’."
- MS23 AO2: "the sense of joy and celebration in ‘Before You Were Mine’"; AO3: "ideas about possession/ownership in the parent/child relationship", "ideas about how parenthood impacts upon sense of self/individual identity", "possible ideas about the impact of parenthood in terms of freedom and responsibility". MS23 suggested partners: Follower, Mother Any Distance, Eden Rock, Walking Away.
- MS25: "admiration for an adult family figure, such as ‘Before You Were Mine’"; "use of memory/incidents as symbolic of ideas, such as ‘Before You Were Mine’".

## Gaps and conflicts

1. Duffy's comment reached only through the University of Glasgow resource (which says it was "published on Sheer Poetry"); original not found. Treated as one route.
2. Mother's name: Duffy says "May"; poets.org says "Mary Black". Not a contradiction for the guide (we use only Duffy's words) but flagged.
3. PMT claims (mother died 2005; Guardian quote about Rapture; WWII "separated" generations; Monroe suicide reading; "right path" misquote) NOT verified, not used. AQA warns students not to read the mother as dead in the poem.
4. "close" and Gorbals birth: GLA only.
5. Portobello: GLA does not say which Portobello; the Edinburgh seaside one is the obvious match but the identification is OUR READING (one gazetteer).
