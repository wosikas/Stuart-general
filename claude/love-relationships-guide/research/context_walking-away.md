## 3. Form (our count from anth.txt)

- 20 lines, four five-line stanzas (quintains). Rhyme ABACA in every stanza: day/play/away; see/free/be; away/convey/clay; so/show/go. B and C lines unrhymed (turning/satellite; school/one; stem/scorching; roughly/away).
- "away" ends lines 5, 11 and 19; "walking away" = line 7 and line 19.
- Enjambment across stanza 1 to 2 ("drifting away / Behind a scatter of boys"); dashes lines 1, 3, 14, 18.
- No metre claim made (no source opened).
