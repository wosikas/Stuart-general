# Love and Relationships: AQA-backed theme tags per poem

Source: built only from `lr/exam_materials_lr.md`, which holds the verbatim AQA question papers (QP), mark-scheme indicative content (MS) and examiners' reports (ER). Each phrase below was re-checked against the extracted text of the downloaded AQA PDFs (`dl/*.txt`, `pmt/*.txt`).

**Rule applied (same as `pc/theme_tags.md`).** A poem gets a theme only when:
- (a) an AQA question names the poem with that focus, or
- (b) an MS or ER bullet names the poem in connection with that idea.

When a bullet lists a poem as a partner, the tag follows the bullet's own idea. A bullet that names only a method ("first person", "direct address", "dialect", "metaphor") gives no theme.

Markers:
- **(indirect)**: the bullet sits in that poem's own question and refers to it without naming it (for example, "the tape measure" in the Mother, any distance question). These rows are kept but left out of the strict theme map.
- **(mapping)**: AQA's words are close to, but not identical with, the theme label. The mapping is my own, and it is flagged so it can be reviewed.

Text in quotation marks is copied verbatim from AQA.

**Source keys:** QP = question paper · SMS = Specimen MS · MS17 / MS18 / MS19 / MS24 / MS25 = June mark schemes (PMT copies of AQA PDFs) · MS-N20 = Nov 2020 MS (June 2020 paper) · MS-N21 = Nov 2021 8702/1P MS · MS-J22 = June 2022 8702/1P MS · MS23 = June 2023 MS · ER-N20, ER-N21, ER-J22, ER23 = examiners' reports.

Theme vocabulary: Romantic love, Desire, Rejection and heartbreak, Separation and distance, Family (parent and child), Memory, Time, Nature, Power and control, Possession, Loss, Longing, Commitment, Growing up, Identity, Place and home.

**Takeaway (from the table, not stated by AQA):** AQA's suggested partners almost always stay inside the same strand. ## Gaps and conflicts
- GAP: these vocabulary themes have **no direct AQA backing** for any poem: Possession (indirect only, Before You Were Mine), Loss (none after the verify pass). Power and control has only a weak mapping (Mother, any distance) plus indirect links (The Farmer's Bride). Desire, Rejection and heartbreak, Separation and distance, Commitment and Longing rest partly or wholly on my mappings (*). A guide must label any other link as its own reading.
- GAP: AQA never links When We Two Parted to heartbreak or separation, Porphyria's Lover to possession or control, or Eden Rock to death or loss, even though these are common readings. AQA's documents simply do not say so.
- GAP: Letters From Yorkshire appears in one AQA document only (SMS), and that document reads it as presenting a "parent". Check this against the poem.
- GAP: there are no examiners' reports for 2017, 2018, 2019, 2024 or 2025, so tags from those years rest on mark schemes only.
- MAPPING CHOICES TO REVIEW: "ending of a relationship" and "sadness" → Rejection and heartbreak (Neutral Tones); "lasting nature" and "more established relationships" → Commitment; "in his absence" → Separation (Sonnet 29; ER-J22 records this as one student reading); "constriction" → Power and control; "the woman their mother was before she was a mother" → Identity.
- TITLE SPELLINGS vary: "Mother, any distance" (QP) / "Mother, Any Distance" (MS, and the June 2019 QP poem list); "Letters From Yorkshire" / "Letters from Yorkshire"; "Sonnet 29" / "Sonnet 29 – I think of thee!" / "Sonnet 29 – ‘I think of thee!’". They are kept verbatim inside quotes.
